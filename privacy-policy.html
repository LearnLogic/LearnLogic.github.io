1. Introduction

LearnLogic ("we", "us", or "our") operates the 11+ Practice Makes Perfect mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

We are committed to protecting the privacy of children and complying with applicable privacy laws, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR).

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you register for the App:

  • Child's first name — to personalise the learning experience
  • Child's date of birth — to calculate the target exam year
  • Parent or guardian's full name
  • Parent or guardian's email address — used to log in and for account communication
  • Selected subjects — English, Maths, Verbal Reasoning, Non-Verbal Reasoning
  • Practice session data — questions answered, scores, time taken, and session history

We do not collect location data, payment card details, or any other personal information beyond what is listed above.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Personalise the child's learning experience
  • Track practice session progress and display analytics to parents
  • Send account-related emails such as password reset requests
  • Improve the App and its content

We do not use your information for advertising purposes and we do not sell your data to third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App is designed for use by children aged 7–12 under the supervision of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. All account registration is completed by a parent or guardian.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child without parental consent, please contact us at staranalytical1@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider. We implement the following security measures:

  • Passwords are securely hashed and never stored in plain text
  • Parent PINs are stored as cryptographic hashes
  • Row-Level Security ensures users can only access their own data
  • All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider
  • When required by law or to protect our legal rights

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at staranalytical1@gmail.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

8. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at staranalytical1@gmail.com.

9. Cookies

Our App does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

10. Third Party Services

Our App uses the following third party services:

  • Supabase — database and authentication (supabase.com/privacy)
  • Expo / EAS — app build and distribution (expo.dev/privacy)
